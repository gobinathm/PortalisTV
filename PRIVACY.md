# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-22_

**Portalis TV collects nothing.** The developers receive no data from the app.

- **No analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, ads or trackers.** The app contains no Google Play Services or Firebase components.
- **What leaves your device:** only what your own IPTV portal requires (for example the MAC address, serial number, device IDs and device/firmware strings you configured). It goes only to the portal URL you entered, and to the stream addresses that portal returns.
- **Device identifiers:** device IDs are generated from a random value per profile. They are never derived from your TV's hardware identifiers.
- **Storage:** profiles and settings stay on your device, encrypted. They are excluded from cloud backups. You can export them yourself as a password-protected file.
- **Crash reports:** none are sent. In a future version you may be able to view and share a redacted report manually.
- **Permissions:** network access only. Start-on-boot is used only if you enable it.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
